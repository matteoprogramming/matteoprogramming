### Thesis

**[thesis-engine](https://github.com/matteoprogramming/thesis-engine)**:
a C11 engine that detects one-way and triangular arbitrage on crypto
spot markets (Binance, Kraken, Coinbase, OKX, Gate.io), live from the
exchanges' WebSockets or replayed from recorded days. Integer arithmetic
throughout, no allocation or clock on the hot path, tens to hundreds of
nanoseconds per quote.

*Measuring Arbitrage Opportunities in Cryptocurrency Spot Markets:
An Empirical Study of One-Way and Triangular Arbitrage*, carried out in
the SystemsLab research group of Sapienza University of Rome.
